Connect TikTok

Authorize Strush Analytics to read the performance metrics of your TikTok account.

What Strush Analytics will access

Strush Analytics is an internal tool of Strush SRLS. It only reads data and never posts or changes anything on your account.

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): display name and avatar.
  • Account statistics (user.info.stats): followers, total likes and number of videos.
  • Your videos (video.list): list of your videos with views, likes, comments and shares.
